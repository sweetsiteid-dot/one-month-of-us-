Happy one month of being together, baby.

Spending this time with you through our everyday words and little conversations has shown me the most beautiful kind of freedom—the freedom to be completely myself. Kean, the way you love me with such genuine sincerity has become the safest place for me to rest, a place where I can pour out my stories without ever being afraid of being judged.

You have never asked me to become someone else just to make you happy. In every warm greeting and every little moment we share, you make me feel accepted exactly as I am; loved so deeply even when I feel far from perfect. The sincerity in your heart has become the sweetest comfort, making my days feel a little more peaceful.

Before our first month comes to an end, there is something I want to let flow straight into your heart. Thank you for everything, my love. Thank you for everything you have brought into my life— for your time, your little acts of care, your incredible acceptance, and all the sincerity you have given me without limits.

Thank you for sharing your world with me and allowing me to simply be your little sweetheart, just as I am, in every part of your days.

Happy one month, baby. I hope this is only the beginning of many more beautiful months with you.

I love you, Kean. 🤍

With Love,
